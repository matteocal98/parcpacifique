How it is calculated

(purchase price − estimated resale value + spending) ÷ (today's reading − reading at purchase)

The loss of value is what you paid, minus what you could get for it today. Your spending since purchase is added, then divided by the hours or kilometres done while the machine has been yours.

Bought second-hand

Only your own use counts. A machine bought at 3,000 h and now showing 5,400 h has done 2,400 h with you: dividing by 5,400 h would give a cost that is too low.

Comparing two costs fairly

To compare two machines, or a cost with a rental rate, count the same expenses on both sides: fuel, insurance, transport, operator.

KALEA works out this cost machine by machine from your maintenance records: see the CMMS software.

Frequently asked questions

Are my figures sent or saved?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing you type is transmitted. Only the page visit is counted, without cookies.

What should I enter as the resale value?

The price you think you could get today. It is an estimate: the result moves with it. If left empty, the whole purchase price is counted.

Should fuel be counted?

Your choice, as long as you do the same for every machine you compare. The field is optional.
