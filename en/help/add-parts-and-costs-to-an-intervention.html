Add parts and costs to an intervention

When your plan includes cost tracking, the intervention window has an optional block, “Intervention costs & TCO”. It is used to note what the service cost: labour, parts, consumables.

  1. 1

    Step 1 : Find the costs block

    Open “Log an intervention” (see log an intervention) and scroll down to “Intervention costs & TCO”. It is marked “Optional”: you can skip it.

  2. 2

    Step 2 : Note the labour

    Enter “Labour hours”. If an hourly rate is saved for your company, the cost is suggested: you can correct it. Then tap “Add”: the amount is added to the parts and consumables total, and the two fields go back to empty. You can repeat it for a second person.

  3. 3

    Step 3 : Add the parts

    Tap “+ Add a part”: the catalogue opens, you tick the parts used, then confirm. With no catalogue, a free line is added. On each line, correct the quantity and the unit price (the catalogue price is only a suggestion). “Remove this part” removes a line.

  4. 4

    Step 4 : Or read an invoice from a photo

    Tap “Scan an invoice” (“AI OCR”) and photograph the invoice. KALEA reads the amount and adds it to the total: « Montant lu : … — vérifie avant d'enregistrer. » Always check it. Reading needs a connection; otherwise, type the amount yourself.

  5. 5

    Step 5 : Take the part out of stock (if available) and confirm

    If the stock module is included in your plan and you have the right to use it, a “Take from stock” box appears on each catalogue part line: tick it and choose the “Store”. KALEA checks that there is enough stock. The total can be edited. Finally click the confirm button: the total is shown next to it.

Good to know

  • The “Total parts & consumables” is recalculated on every line, but you can also type it directly (for example for a single global invoice).
  • If stock runs short at the time of saving, the stock withdrawal is refused but the part is not lost: it stays in the intervention with its price. KALEA then tells you to record the withdrawal by hand in the stock.
  • Stock withdrawal is offered to “Administrator”, to “Director” and to “Management access”.
  • If the “Scan a part” button is present, it reads the QR code on the stock label and adds the part.
