Create a work order from a breakdown or a machine page

No need to retype everything: from a machine's page or from a reported breakdown, KALEA prepares the order for you.

  1. 1

    Step 1 : From a machine page: open the page

    Open the machine's page (“Maintenance plan & history”) and click “Create an order”. If the plan has late or soon-due tasks, they are already ticked.

  2. 2

    Step 2 : Check the suggested order

    The “New work order” window opens on the machine. Adjust the tasks, the “Priority” and the person in “Assigned to”.

  3. 3

    Step 3 : From a breakdown: open the list

    Open “Reported faults”, offered in the “Quick actions” (the “+” button of the bottom bar). On an open breakdown, click “Create a work order”.

  4. 4

    Step 4 : Complete and create

    The breakdown's description becomes the task “Repair: …”. The priority is suggested from the severity: Urgent for a blocking breakdown, High for an important one, Normal otherwise. Correct it if needed, then click “Create the order”.

Good to know

  • An order created from a breakdown has the origin “From a breakdown”; one created from a machine page with a plan has “From a maintenance plan”.
  • These buttons are reserved for the manager and management access, and only appear if work orders are available on your plan.
  • The “Create a work order” button is only offered on breakdowns that are still open.
  • For the management access level, the “+” button's sheet is called “Enter”.

A question?

Can't find the answer? In the app, open “Help & support” and write to us: the team replies in the same screen. You can also email support@kalea.pro.
