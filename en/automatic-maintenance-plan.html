Automatic maintenance plan: upload the manual, KALEA builds the plan

Hunting for the right page in forty manuals, copying out intervals, forgetting a service… KALEA does that work for you: you upload the manufacturer manual, automatic reading builds a maintenance plan from it, and you stay in control to review and correct it.

Free trial See pricing

In three steps

  • Import the manual. Upload the machine's manufacturer PDF. A photo of the plate or logbook is enough to find the right document; you can add the model and year.
  • Validate the proposed plan. KALEA extracts the tasks, the intervals and the tracking unit (hours, kilometres or months), first service included. You review, correct if needed, then confirm.
  • Track, then order. Read the meter whenever you like: the schedule updates, reminders go out before the due date, and the parts list is grouped by family.

What KALEA extracts from the manual

  • the recurring intervals, in hours, kilometres or calendar time;
  • the service sheets and specifications (oils, filters, greases…);
  • the manufacturer's recommendations, task by task.

You stay in control

The plan is never locked: every task can be edited, and each one can have its own due date. A machine follows its hour meter and its calendar at the same time (dual tracking): the first interval reached triggers the alert and prepares the service checklist. The proposed plan does not replace the manufacturer's recommendations or a qualified professional's advice.

What if the manual can't be found?

No time, or you can't find the logbook for your model? Send us a photo of the manufacturer's plate: we find the manual and set up the plan in the application. This service costs €29 including tax per plan, payable once the plan has been delivered and agreed with you.

Going further

Once the plan is in place: construction equipment tracking (meters, QR codes, breakdown reporting) and the full CMMS (parts, stock, costs).

Frequently asked questions

Which documents can I import?

The manufacturer's manual or service logbook as a PDF. A photo of the manufacturer's plate helps find the right document.

Is the generated plan reliable?

It is based on your manual, but you must review and validate it before using it. KALEA does not replace the manufacturer's recommendations or a qualified professional's advice.

Can I edit the plan?

Yes: tasks, intervals and tracking units stay editable, machine by machine.

How many manual analyses are included?

Each plan includes a monthly quota of logbook analyses (10 on the Free plan). Details are in the comparison table of the pricing section.
